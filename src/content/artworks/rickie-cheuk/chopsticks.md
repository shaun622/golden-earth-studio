This exquisite pair of chopsticks is handcrafted by Ricky Cheuk in his London Studio. Created from metal reclaimed from an old sink salvaged during the demolition of a London residential home.

Ricky melted down the salvaged material and meticulously carved and polished each chopstick, resulting in a pair that is not only visually striking but also perfectly balanced in the hand.

The smooth, tactile finish and subtle weight distribution elevate everyday use into a mindful, sensory experience. A functional object with a rich narrative, these chopsticks are as meaningful as they are beautiful.
