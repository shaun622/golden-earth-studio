Inspired by considering the elements, in particular for this piece; earth, wind and fire. Each of the twelve zodiac signs belongs to one of four classical elements: Fire, Earth, Air, and Water. The combination within the artwork of earth, wind and fire form a dynamic mix of physical grounding and passionate energy.

Paired with Iridescent Marcasite.
