**Bulls Matter** depicts a mythological encounter between a mighty bull and a delicate female, exploring themes of surrender and coexistence. This piece serves as a metaphor for escapism, transcendence, struggle, or death. Made from London construction clay, its textured surface enhances the tension and harmony between these contrasting beings.
