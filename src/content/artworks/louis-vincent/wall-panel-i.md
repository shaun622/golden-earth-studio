Louis Vincent invites a second glance with the wall panel’s unexpected surface.

Made from reclaimed wood panels, the piece is treated with clay sourced from London construction sites and sealed with natural lacquer, resulting in a rich, earthy texture that evokes the depth and visual weight of another material entirely.
Through this transformation, Vincent highlights the quiet beauty of reclaimed matter, offering a piece that speaks to both process and place.
