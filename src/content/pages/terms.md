## Thank you for considering Golden Earth Studio for your art inquiries. Here are some important details you should be aware of before proceeding:

Contact and Sales

- We facilitate art sales via email inquiries. To inquire about purchasing a piece of art, please email us goldenearthstudio@outlook.com

- By contacting us via email regarding an art piece, you express your interest in purchasing the artwork.

Payment

- Payment details will be discussed and agreed upon via email correspondence.

- We accept payment via methods that we'll communicate through email.

Enquiries and Communication

- When you inquire about a piece of art, please provide your full name, contact email, and any specific details you'd like to discuss.

- We will reply to your email inquiry within a reasonable time frame to discuss details, pricing, and payment arrangements.

Returns

- Due to the nature of bespoke art works, all sales are considered final and non-returnable.

Privacy

- We respect your privacy and will use your provided information only for the purpose of art inquiries and transactions.

- We do not share your information with third parties and follow applicable privacy laws.

Contact

For any inquiries, assistance, or to start the purchase process, please email us at goldenearthstudio@outlook.com.

Thank you for considering Golden Earth Studio for your art needs.
