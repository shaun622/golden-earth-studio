31| 01 | 2022 A 4 metre deep basement has been carved out of the property site as a result of the excavation process. This has revealed the two different layers of clay. The upper portions consist of alternating clays and sands, known as the Claygate Beds. The lower half is blue London clay. These formations are from the Eocene age, dating back to 50million years old, where quartz is the most abundant material.
