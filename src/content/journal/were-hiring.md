Golden Earth Studio is looking for a Fine Art Sales Specialist (part-time) to support the growth of our gallery, which represents artists transforming construction waste through art, craft, and material innovation.

This role is ideal for someone experienced in the fine art world who is passionate about sustainability and excited by the idea of bringing waste-derived materials back into living environments and interiors.

The role:
– You will focus on driving sales and building relationships with interior designers, architects, collectors, and private clients, acting as a key link between artists and clients.

Key responsibilities include:
– Driving sales through designers, architects, and collectors
– Liaising between artists and clients
– Building and maintaining strong industry relationships
– Supporting the growth of Golden Earth Studio with a clear sales focus
