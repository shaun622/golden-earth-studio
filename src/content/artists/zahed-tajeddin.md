Born in Aleppo, Syria, one of the world’s oldest inhabited cities, Zahed draws deeply from its archaeological and cultural heritage. His work explores identity, history, and memory, shaped by Syria’s rich material culture and recent upheavals. Zahed’s precise ceramic practice reflects his archaeological expertise, creating richly textured, tactile forms. He masterfully transformed London’s construction clay into elegant, refined artworks, blending ancient sensibility with contemporary craft.
