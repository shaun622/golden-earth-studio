Louis Vincent’s studio practice is defined by a profound engagement with materiality and form. Working primarily with hardwoods and natural lacquer, he explores the intrinsic beauty of raw, tactile surfaces. The introduction of construction clay, a typically discarded material, adds a compelling conceptual and textural dimension to his work. Through this transformation, Vincent elevates the overlooked, crafting objects of quiet elegance and desire.
