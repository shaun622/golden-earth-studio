Grace Rose Watts is a UK-based multi-disciplinary artist whose work delves into the essence of spirituality, design and wellbeing in the pursuit of happiness and peace. Guided by a study practice across cosmology and archival research, her creations blend historical influences with contemporary expression. Whether through visual art, objects, or photography, her work bridges the gap between introspection and design. Transforming her philosophical inquiries into evocative, timeless pieces.

Working with Golden Earth Studio, Grace has taken the opportunity to use London clay as a base material and pigment. The gallery’s ethos of circularity and connecting the home and self to nature works in harmony with Grace’s own philosophy and daily practice.
