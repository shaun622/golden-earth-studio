Claymoon by Adam Weismann, is a series of hand-formed clay artworks by Adam Weismann that speak of the rhythms of nature, the beauty of imperfection and the processes of deep time. Claymoon holds a particular fascination with the aesthetic and functional properties of clay, and one that began when Adam Weismann, along with his wife Katy Bryce, founded Clayworks in 2010. Clayworks came from a shared love of nature and a belief in natural and sustainable building practices. Weismann and Bryce travelled the world to learn about vernacular traditions of building with clay and whilst initially focused on the integrity and performance of the material, they also saw the capacity of natural materials to affect the way we experience the spaces we inhabit. This fascination has continued into the work of Claymoon, which also passively effect the environment in the space in which they are shown.
