Jo Pearl trained in ceramics at Central Saint Martins, launching her hybrid practice: breathing life into clay, she often combines clay animation with ceramic sculpture, or puts her ceramic elements in motion in kinetic sculpture. Her claymations have won several awards, including Best Short Film at the 2023 International Ceramics Festival in Aberystwyth, and were shortlisted for Earth Photo 2024. She is a member of the Royal Society of Sculptors and exhibited in Somerset House’s 2025 landmark exhibition SOIL: The World at our Feet, where her work was showcased on the exhibition poster. One of her latest projects, Dirty Secret, was commissioned by the EU for a touring exhibition entitled Soil Art Tales, which is being shown at the Serralves Art Foundation in Porto, Villa Altieri in Rome and the National Gallery of Bulgaria in Sofia in 2026–27. Selected works are currently on display in Living Labyrinths: Art and Fungi at the Royal Albert Memorial Museum and Art Gallery in Exeter (October 2026 – February 2027), as well as Edge: Where the Land Meets the Sea at Hastings Contemporary (September 2026 – January 2027). A new soil-focused exhibition at MUDEC in Milan will also be showing her work in spring 2027.
