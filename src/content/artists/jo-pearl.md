Jo Pearl is a multi-disciplinary artist, with a practice that combines clay animation and ceramic sculpture with a particular focus on soil microbes and ecological concerns. She is a member of the Royal Society of Sculptors, has exhibited in the UK and internationally, and her clay animated shorts have been shown and garnered awards at film festivals in the UK and abroad. Pearl’s work has been featured in exhibitions at Somerset House, Serralves Art Foundation in Porto, and is currently on display at Hastings Contemporary and the Royal Albert Memorial Museum and Art Gallery in Exeter, and will be on show at MUDEC in Milan and the National Gallery of Bulgaria in 2027.
