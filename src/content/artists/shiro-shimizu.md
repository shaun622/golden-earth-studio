Shiro Shimizu’s ceramic practice begins with searching for clay and digging it from the earth himself.

Shimizu explores clay from a variety of locations, including places close to home in Shiga-ken, Japan. He investigates the unique properties and characteristics of each material, and creates works that reveal its individual qualities. From handling the clay and shaping it, to exploring glazes and firing- he values the discoveries, sensations, and shifts in awareness that emerge throughout the creative process.

For Shimizu, making ceramics is not simply about producing a finished object. It's about allowing the energy and sense of life that emerge through his encounters with clay and fire, to become part of the work. Ultimately, to be felt by those who experience it.
